Privacy & Consents

Pulse operates on consented signals only. You control which signals to collect and can revoke any permission instantly.

1. Food Order Receipt Processing

Extracts order delivery time, amount, and broad behavioral categories to identify late-night ordering patterns.

Stored: Delivery timestamp, restaurant name, food items list, estimated amount, behavioral categories.
NOT Stored: Raw receipt text, payment cards, phone numbers, delivery addresses, OTPs.

2. Blood Pressure & Manual Vitals Storage

Stores user-entered blood pressure readings and manual pulse entries to monitor deviations against personal baselines.

Stored: Systolic, diastolic, optional pulse, timestamp, data source.
NOT Stored: Insurance details, clinical diagnosis labels, medication prescriptions.

3. Experimental Camera Pulse Estimation

Uses your phone camera to calculate an experimental pulse estimate via fingertip photoplethysmography (PPG).

Stored: Final estimated BPM, duration, signal quality score.
NOT Stored: Camera video, raw frames, or face recordings. Frames are processed entirely in browser memory and immediately discarded.

4. Typing Rhythm Behavioral Analysis

Measures natural cadence, typing speed, pauses, and backspaces during a short prompt check-in.

Stored: Speed (WPM), error rate, pause frequency, session duration.
NOT Stored: What you typed. Keystrokes are counted in memory; text content is never recorded or sent.
