🚨 Urgent Medical Guidance

Seek urgent medical care immediately for chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting, confusion, or severe headache.

Do not wait for co-pilot signals or app recommendations if you or someone around you experiences acute cardiovascular, neurological, or respiratory distress symptoms.

What Project Pulse Is and Is Not

Clear boundaries protect your health and your autonomy.

Project Pulse is a wellness and behavioral-support tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat medical conditions.

The tool is designed for high-stress professionals to notice patterns in lifestyle routines—such as late-night ordering, typing cadence pauses, and resting pulse variations—and suggest gentle, non-pharmacological micro-actions.

Never ignore professional medical advice, alter prescription medications, or delay seeking medical attention based on any observation or metric shown within Project Pulse.

Signal / Feature What Pulse Does What Pulse Never Does
Camera Pulse Estimates heart rate using optical color variations (experimental wellness metric). Does not compute HRV, SpO2, blood pressure, or diagnose arrhythmias.
Blood Pressure Tracks self-reported readings against simplified cut-offs to observe routine trends. Does not diagnose hypertension or recommend medications/dosages.
Typing Cadence Measures rhythm, speed, and pause frequency during voluntary test passages. Does not evaluate cognitive impairment, store keystroke text, or log private messages.
Food Orders Identifies late-night delivery timing to highlight disruption to rest routines. Never counts calories, tracks weight, assigns dietary guilt, or claims causality.

Privacy & Data Autonomy

Your data remains under your absolute control.

  • All 4 signal channels (Camera, Behavior, Orders, Health data) require explicit, affirmative opt-in consent before processing.
  • Raw camera video feeds and individual keystroke characters are never stored or transmitted to the database.
  • Raw receipt text is discarded immediately after in-memory parsing to prevent storing personal identifiers, addresses, or phone numbers.
  • You can export all data as an open ZIP archive or permanently wipe your records at any time from Settings.
  • This design aligns with the fundamental principles of data minimization and purposeful processing, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. (Note: this is not formal legal advice).
